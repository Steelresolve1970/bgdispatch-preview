What we plan to collect (not live yet)

  • Contact & Suggestions form: what you type (message and topic, plus your name and email only if you give them) and a scrambled form of your IP address to stop spam. We use it only to read and answer your message and keep it for about a year.
  • Email newsletter: your email address, with double opt-in and an unsubscribe link in every email.
  • Member accounts and forum: username, email, password (hashed), posts you write, and settings. Accounts are free; there is no paid membership today.
  • Advertising and analytics: if we add ads or analytics, partners may set cookies or similar tech to measure and personalize ads. We will ask for consent where the law requires it and will update this policy and our Cookie notice first.

Fan accounts (forum, Grade the Team, Pick 'Em and Trivia)

  • What we store: your username, email, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password (we never see or store the password itself), display name, favorite-player flair, short bio, profile picture (if you upload one; it's resized on your device and checked there for explicit content before it's sent, and a moderator reviews it before it's shown), your post count, your posts, reactions, reports, player grades, Pick 'Em picks and trivia results.
  • What others see: your display name, flair and what you post. Your email is never shown. Grades are shown only as averages.
  • Staying signed in: when you sign in we set one essential cookie (bgd_s) so you stay signed in for up to a year, even after you leave and come back. It's only used for signing in, not for ads. Signing out removes it.
  • Safety: to stop spam and abuse we count requests over short time windows using a scrambled form of your IP address.
  • Where it's kept: on Cloudflare, our hosting provider.
  • Your choices: you can change your display name and flair anytime and ask us to delete your account and data. We don't send email yet; if we add verification or password-reset emails later, we'll only use your email for your account.

How we use data

To run and secure the site, remember your choices, send emails you asked for, run accounts and the forum, and (if launched) show ads. We do not sell personal information today. The site is free; we do not process membership payments.
