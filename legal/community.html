Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The Black & Gold Dispatch forum is a place for Steelers fans to talk football. Argue about the depth chart, vent after a loss, celebrate a win. Just keep it about the game, not the person you're arguing with.

The short version

  • Be a good teammate. Disagree with ideas, not people. No insults, threats, harassment or hate of any kind.
  • Keep it clean-ish. Our filter masks common swears and blocks slurs. Don't try to get around it.
  • Facts are facts, rumors are rumors. Don't make up injuries, arrests, quotes or "sources." Label opinions and rumors.
  • Link, don't copy. Share a link and a sentence in your own words. Don't paste whole articles, paywalled text, photos or video you don't own.
  • No spam. No ads, betting promos, scams, or posting the same thing over and over.
  • Protect privacy. Never post someone's personal info (address, phone, workplace, etc.), including your own.
  • Be yourself. Don't pretend to be a player, coach, reporter, moderator or another fan. Our bot is always labeled BOT.
  • 13 and up. You must be at least 13 to have an account.

Game Day threads and Grade the Team

Game Day threads open on their own the day before each game, and post-game threads open at the final whistle. Trash talk about the other team is fine; personal attacks on other fans aren't. The Grade the Team poll lets each account grade each player once per game. Please grade honestly and use one account only.

Reporting and moderation

See something that breaks these rules? Tap ⚑ Report on the post. Moderators review reports and may hide or delete posts, lock threads, and suspend or ban accounts. We try to be fair and consistent, but moderators make the final call. Repeated or serious violations, including copyright infringement, can lead to a permanent ban.

Profile pictures

Pick one of our preset avatars or upload your own. Only upload images you own or have permission to use: no logos, no copyrighted photos, no explicit or hateful images. Every uploaded picture is checked automatically on your device and then approved by a moderator before anyone else can see it. Until then, others see your initials. Moderators can remove any picture, and you can report one with the Report button on a post.

Repeat rule-breakers

When a moderator rejects a picture or deletes a post for breaking these rules, that's a strike. Three strikes means an automatic 30-day posting ban, and moderators can ban sooner for serious cases.

Copyright and takedowns

Only post what you have the right to share. If you think something here uses your work without permission, tell us through our DMCA / Copyright page or tap Report on the post. We'll take down infringing content when we get a valid request, and we close the accounts of people who keep doing it.

Your account

Keep your password private. You can change your display name and favorite-player flair anytime. Email verification and password reset by email are coming soon; until then, ask a moderator in the forum if you're locked out.
