About Black & Gold Dispatch

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Black & Gold Dispatch is an unofficial Pittsburgh Steelers fan site. It is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by the Pittsburgh Steelers, the NFL, or any team or player.

Who runs it

It's run by David Duffy, a Steelers fan, as a hobby project. The forum is moderated by David and a few volunteer moderators.

What's here

News links (headlines that go straight to the original publishers), schedule and scores, our own player grades and opinions, depth chart and injury info, draft tools, the fan forum, Pick 'Em and trivia. Grades, previews and opinion pieces are our own takes, not facts.

Where the data comes from

  • Schedule, scores, stats, rankings, depth chart and injury report: nflverse (CC-BY 4.0).
  • College prospect stats: CollegeFootballData.com.
  • News: headlines link to the publishers who own them. We never copy article text or publisher photos.
  • Some history facts are adapted from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • Expert picks: we link to the experts' own pages instead of copying them.
