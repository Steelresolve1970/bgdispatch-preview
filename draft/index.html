Availability method (estimate): Estimate, not a prediction: a prospect's real draft slot ~ Normal(mean = our board rank, sd = (4 + 0.30*rank) * position factor). Chance he is still there at pick k = P(slot > k-1) = 1 - Phi((k - 0.5 - rank) / sd). Position factor widens the spread for positions whose draft range is historically less predictable (QB 1.25; RB/LB/S/IOL/TE 1.10; others 1.0).
